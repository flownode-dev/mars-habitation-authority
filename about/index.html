The Authority

The Mars Habitation Authority publishes and maintains the reference standards for sustaining human life on the Martian surface.

01

Mandate

The Authority's mandate is narrow and specific: keep the technical standards for human survival on Mars accurate, current, and freely available, independent of any single mission, vehicle, or operator. This manual does not endorse particular hardware or vendors. It documents what is physically required to keep people alive and functioning on the Martian surface, drawing only on figures that are established, published, and verifiable — from planetary science, from landed-mission instrumentation, and from human-spaceflight life-support engineering.

Where the underlying science is unsettled or a figure is only a design target rather than a proven result, this manual says so rather than presenting it as fact.

02

Scope

Eight life-support systems — atmosphere, water, radiation, thermal, food, health, communications, and habitat — form the baseline this manual documents. Supporting reference material (hazard notices, glossary, orientation guidance, and calculation tools) is maintained alongside the systems reference for practical day-to-day use.

03

Origin

The Authority's public information portal was established in 2026, on the occasion of the public-market listing of a private spaceflight company: Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SpaceX) priced its initial public offering and began trading on Nasdaq under the ticker SPCX in June 2026. That listing marked the point at which the capital and cadence required for sustained Mars logistics became a matter of public financial record rather than internal roadmap — and the point at which a standing, public, non-proprietary reference for surface survival became a practical necessity rather than a speculative exercise.

04

Document status

System status: operational · Manual actively maintained · See the systems index for the current reference baseline

Note: there are currently no residents on Mars. This manual is maintained in advance of need.
